    How to Play

    Swap two neighbouring balls to line up three or more of a kind.

    Four in a line earns a Striker — it sweeps a whole row or column.

    Five in a line earns a Match Ball — it clears every ball of one kind.

    An L or T shape earns a Scrum — it blows out everything around it.
